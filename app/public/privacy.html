Privacy Policy

Sabeel Institute Kanban · last updated 16 October 2026

Sabeel Institute Kanban is a private task-management application used by staff and volunteers of Sabeel Institute. It is not a public service. Only the institute’s own @oursabeel.com Workspace accounts can sign in, and an administrator has to approve your account before you can see anything.

What we collect

InformationWhere it comes fromWhy
Your name, email address and Google profile picture Your Google Workspace account, when you sign in To identify you to colleagues on shared boards
The work you enter — cards, comments, attached files You It is the content of the application
Crash and error diagnostics The app, automatically So faults can be found and fixed

Crash reports carry your internal account identifier and nothing else — no name, no email address. That is enforced in the application's code, not by policy alone.

We do not use advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or share your information with anyone for their own purposes.

Who else can see it

Three service providers process this information on the institute's behalf, under agreements requiring them to protect it to the same standard set out here, and to use it only to provide their service:

  • Google — sign-in.
  • Google Cloud and Firebase — where the application and its data are hosted, in the United States.
  • Sentry — crash and error diagnostics.

On an iPhone, a notification’s title and text are delivered through Apple’s push notification service, as every iPhone notification is.

How long we keep it

Work recorded in the application — cards, comments, attached files, and the record of who created or changed them — belongs to Sabeel Institute rather than to the individual who entered it, and is retained as an organisational record. That remains true after somebody leaves, in the same way a document keeps its author's name.

Your account is a different thing: your sign-in credentials, your profile and your contact details are removed when your account is removed.

Deleting your account

Email privacy@oursabeel.com from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm and act within 30 days.

Deleting your account removes your ability to sign in, your profile, your contact details and your notification settings. It does not remove the organisational records described above, which the institute retains.

You can also ask us at the same address what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct it.

Children

This application is not intended for and is not offered to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and tell account holders by email.
